Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

What we collect

When you purchase a license we store the information you provide at checkout: the name or username you enter, the plan purchased and the license key issued. We do not ask for, and never store, payment card details.

When you use the software, license data (key, plan, activation date and device identifier) is stored locally and used only to verify your license and keep the product working.

Your seller data

BoostPilot runs on your machine using your own logged-in seller session. Your marketplace credentials and seller data stay on your device — we do not upload or share them with any external server.

Discord

If you join our Discord server or open a support ticket, we use the data you share there (username, messages) only to help you with setup and support.

Analytics and cookies

This website is served directly by the application and sets no third-party cookies. We do not use third-party analytics trackers.

Data retention and deletion

We keep purchase records for as long as needed to operate the service and comply with legal obligations. To request deletion of your data, contact us on Discord.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are effective when posted on this page.
