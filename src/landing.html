FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What exactly does BoostPilot do? +
BoostPilot is a Windows tool for Eldorado.gg sellers. It monitors the seller hub, detects new boosting requests in real time and prepares your offer automatically — price, delivery time and welcome message — so you just review and confirm. It runs 24/7 on your PC.
Do I need technical knowledge to set it up? +
Not at all. Install the app, log in with your license key, connect your Eldorado seller account and pick your settings. Everything is controlled from a clean dashboard — no command line, no JSON editing.
Which games and order types does it support? +
Currently 11 games: Valorant, League of Legends, Brawl Stars, EA Sports FC, Clash Royale, Rocket League, Fortnite, R6 Siege, Marvel Rivals, Apex Legends and Call of Duty. You can toggle rank boost, trophies, placements and more independently per game. More games are added over time — request yours on Discord.
Can I customize the pricing? +
Yes, fully. You set the price per tier and per game, plus multipliers for Duo, Solo queue and Stream, and skip toggles for anything you don't want to take. BoostPilot applies your exact rules to every offer.
Can I run it 24/7? +
Yes. BoostPilot is lightweight and built to run for days — just leave your PC on with the app running and it keeps detecting and responding to requests while you sleep.
How does the license work if I change PCs? +
Your license is tied to your hardware so only one machine runs it at a time. If you upgrade or move to a new PC, open a ticket on Discord and we reset it for free — no questions asked.
What happens when Eldorado updates its site? +
We monitor the marketplace and push fixes whenever a change breaks something. Fixes typically go out within hours. All plan holders get updates for the duration of their license.
Do you offer refunds? +
Due to the nature of digital software licenses, we don't offer refunds once a key has been activated. But we provide full support through Discord to make sure everything works on your setup, and you can start with the affordable 3-day plan to test before committing.
Will my Eldorado seller account be affected? +
BoostPilot works with your own logged-in seller session — the same account you use manually. It doesn't use shared sessions or external servers. As always, the health of your account depends on your order history, so keep your cancellation rate reasonable.
